Live working numbers as of May 22, 2026. The full model and current cap table are walked through on the founder call — figures below are the headline view.

Revenue model (next 12–18 months)

Three parallel revenue streams. The CE course is the near-term bridge; B2C subscription and enterprise pilots are the larger lines.

CE course — CRNAs (AANA pathway)

Addressable: ~65,000 licensed CRNAs + ~2,000 new graduates per year. At 1% capture × $199, $1M+ annualized. AANA accreditation in submission.

CE course — RNs (ANCC pathway)

Addressable: ~4,000,000 licensed nurses (ANCC accreditation, parallel pathway adopted May 22). At ¼% capture × $199, $2M annualized.

B2C subscription (Pausality app)

Apple Watch + iPhone, paying customers in operating rooms today. Subscription pricing, scaling with marketing spend post-close.

Enterprise pilots

Active conversations with Mayo Department of Surgery; pilot one-pager out to MUSC (May 19, 2026). Pilots ~2–3 months out.

Numbers above exclude: grant funding, non-dilutive financing, and any partnership revenue.
