The problem

High-performance professionals are expected to stay calm, sharp, and emotionally available under sustained pressure. Most stress-management tools are designed for long sessions, quiet environments, or reflective moments that happen outside the flow of real work.

For clinicians, founders, operators, and leaders the need is different. A trusted way to pause, regulate, and return to performance in one minute. Pausality is built around that moment.

The product

Pausality delivers 21 progressive one-minute breathing sessions that build interoceptive awareness, nervous-system regulation, and confidence through repeated practice. Each session is short enough to use before a case, between meetings, after a difficult interaction, or during a transition point in the day.

The app pairs guided breathing with real-time biometric feedback on Apple Watch and iPhone. Users see how their body responds while they practice. Stress becomes something to notice, train, and improve over time, not an abstract feeling to push through.
